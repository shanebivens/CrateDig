CrateDig

Send one in

A record almost nobody has heard, or nobody has played in years. It does not have to be great, and it does not have to be something you like.

Put yourself on the participant list. Picks pulled from your playlist credit you when they land in a drop.

What happens next

  1. The form hands you a GitHub issue with every field already filled in.
  2. You press Create. That is the only step on their side.
  3. A bot reads it, files it into the repository, and replies within a minute.

Submitting needs a free GitHub account, which is the one piece of friction left. Nothing here ever asks for a password to a music service, a cookie, or access to your listening account.

CrateDig links to music. It does not host it or copy it. Every recording belongs to whoever made it.
