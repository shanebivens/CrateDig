Read this before your first pull request. It applies to code, to submission

## Why this exists

CrateDig is released under the AGPL-3.0 and may also be offered under a
commercial license (see [`COMMERCIAL-LICENSE.md`](COMMERCIAL-LICENSE.md)).
That second option only works if one party can license the entire codebase.
Without this agreement, every outside contribution would be permanently
AGPL-only and would have to be torn out and rewritten before any part of the
project could be licensed on other terms.

Getting this on paper before the first outside pull request is much cheaper
than trying to track people down a year from now.

description of your first pull request:

request and describe what you had in mind. Ideas are welcome either way.
