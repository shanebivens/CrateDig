The practice

  1. 1

    Dig

    Find one nobody plays. Send it in. A link is enough, everything else fills itself in. It goes out in your words, with your name on it.

  2. 2

    Fall in

    Press it. Do not skip. Do not steer. You are not choosing the music. You are choosing the direction.

  3. 3

    Bring one back

    Whatever you found in there, send that too. Somebody else falls into it. Then it is their turn.

Once a week. Then round again.

Bring one back How it works

Starting from nothing

Thirty minutes is smaller than it sounds. Pick one and go.

  • Play a new release in a genre you never touch.
  • Play the thing you assume you will hate.
  • Let one album run all the way through while you do something else.
  • Press Fall in on one track above and do not skip.
  • Ask people for things to put in your thirty minutes. Send them here.

Half of what is worth hearing arrives because somebody handed it to you.

One rule. Do not judge people by what they send in. That is not politeness. It is the whole experiment.
