Thirty minutes a day of music you have never heard. That is the whole practice. Everything here exists to make it easy to keep.

Every track is a doorway, not a playlist. Press Fall in and it plays, then keeps going into whatever the machine decides it is like. One song turns into an afternoon. Do not work through the list. Fall through one of them.

A new drop lands every Monday at 12:01pm Eastern. Five to seven tracks almost nobody plays.

It does not have to be great. It does not have to be something you like. Weird, mainstream, sideways, all of it counts. One rule: do not judge people by what they send in. That misses the point.

The practice

  1. 1

    Dig

    Find one track you think almost nobody has heard, or nobody has played in years. Send it in. It goes out in your words, with your name on it.

  2. 2

    Fall in

    Press Fall in on any track in the drop, or start the radio from it in your own app. Then leave it alone. Where it goes without you steering is the entire point.

  3. 3

    Bring one back

    Whatever turned up in there that you had never heard. Send that in too. It goes into a future drop, somebody else falls into it, and they bring one back. That is the loop closing.

Once a week. Then round again.

Starting from nothing

Thirty minutes is smaller than it sounds and you do not need a plan. Pick one of these and go.

  • Play a new release in a genre you never touch.
  • Play the thing you assume you will hate.
  • Let one album run all the way through while you do something else.
  • Press Fall in on the first track in this drop and do not skip.
  • Ask people for things to put in your thirty minutes, and send them here.

Half of what is worth hearing arrives because somebody handed it to you.

CrateDig links to music. It does not host it or copy it. Every recording belongs to whoever made it.
