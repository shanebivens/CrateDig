Every track here is a doorway, not a playlist. Press Fall in and it plays, then keeps going into whatever the machine decides it is like. One song can turn into an afternoon. You are not meant to work through the list, you are meant to fall through one of them.

A new drop lands every Monday at 12:01pm Eastern. Five to seven tracks almost nobody plays, pulled from what everyone has been finding.

It does not have to be great. It does not even have to be something you like. Weird, mainstream, sideways, all of it counts. One rule: do not judge people by what they send in. That misses the point.

How to dig

  1. 1

    Send in a track

    One you think almost nobody has heard, or nobody has played in years. Four fields. It goes out in your words, with your name on it, in a drop within a week or two.

  2. 2

    Pick one and fall in

    Press Fall in on any track below, or open it in your own app and start the radio from there. Then leave it alone. The point is where it goes without you steering.

  3. 3

    Come back with one or two you found

    Whatever turned up in there that you had never heard. Those go into a future drop, and somebody else falls into them.

Then round again, once a week.

Never done this before

Start small. Thirty minutes a day is the whole habit, and it does not have to be difficult. Put on a random new release in a genre you never touch. Play the thing you assume you will hate. Let one album run all the way through while you do something else.

Or make it somebody else's problem: tell people you are after tracks for a thirty minute a day playlist and send them here to do the same. Half of what is worth hearing arrives because somebody handed it to you.
