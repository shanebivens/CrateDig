Every day, about thirty minutes of music I have never heard, or have not heard in a very long time. That is the whole ritual.

What genre, album, artist, or song do you think almost nobody has heard, or nobody has played in years? It does not have to be great. It does not even have to be something you like. Weird, mainstream, sideways, all of it counts.

Every track here is a doorway, not a playlist. Press Fall in and it plays, then keeps going into whatever the machine thinks it is like. One song can turn into an afternoon.

A new drop lands every Monday at 12:01pm Eastern, five to seven of them, pulled from what everyone has been finding.

One rule: do not judge people by what they submit. Doing that misses the point of the experiment.

Fill in four fields and you are done. The form hands you a filled-out submission, you press one button, and a bot files it into the repository within a minute. No git, no pull request, nothing to learn.

One track at a time. Every drop holds two spots for what people send in, newest first, and your writeup goes out in your words with your name on it.

You send public links, nothing else. No password, no cookie, no access to your listening account, not now and not later.
